All inputs are illustrative assumptions (soil TAW 100 mm, p=0.4, Ky=1.05, potential yield 40 t/ha, farmer application efficiency 65% vs drip 90%, 40 m total head, diesel 0.073 L/m³). The farmer baseline (furrow, every 9 days, 55 mm gross ≈ 7,700 m³/ha) should be confirmed with local survey data. Capex ₹1.1 lakh/ha ≈ drip ₹50k–1.5 lakh/ha + 5 HP solar pump ₹3–3.5 lakh shared across ~6 ha + tank/meters. Capital subsidy 45% is a blend: pump ≈ 30% central + ~10% state, drip 45–55% (PMKSY-PDMC). Both schemes are written for individual farmers, so FPO-hub eligibility must be confirmed. The diesel baseline applies to off-grid farms; for subsidised-grid farms the farmer-cost saving is much smaller. Replace them with Marathwada field data in the pilot. The WPC tiers are a proposed pilot policy, not an existing government rate. Capped at 40% here.
