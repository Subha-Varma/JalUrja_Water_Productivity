📱 JalUrja farmer app

Interactive wireframe: voice-first, Marathi with English subtitles, four big buttons. Tap around, then change the scenario on the right.

Demo scenario (what the engine decided today)

Design decisions

  • Voice first: every screen has a 🔊 button that reads the message aloud (device voice permitting).
  • One decision on the home screen: a single large status with colour and icon and words, so it works for low-literacy and colour-blind users.
  • Four buttons only: Water, Weather, Crop, Payment. No menus, no typing.
  • Numbers in plain units: m³ and ₹, each shown with what it means for the plot.
  • Low connectivity: the same advice is sent as an SMS, and the FPO operator can read it out on a basic phone.
