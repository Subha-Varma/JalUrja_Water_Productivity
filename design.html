Output (kg) comes from FPO weighbridge or aggregation receipts, so the productivity claim is verifiable rather than self-reported. Stack: Python/FastAPI, PostgreSQL + PostGIS for plot polygons.

Roles: Farmer pays and follows advice · FPO / operator owns the hub and collects tariff · JalUrja runs the engine and verification · Government pays WPC only on verified output per m³.
